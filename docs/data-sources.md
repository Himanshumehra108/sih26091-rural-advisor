# Data Sources

Planned sources for the advisory system:

- Census and SECC datasets for demographic and household context.
- NSSO datasets for local pricing and consumption signals.
- OGD and GST datasets for business and economic indicators.
- OpenStreetMap and Nominatim for location and market reach context.

Raw files belong in `server/app/data_pipeline/raw/`; cleaned outputs belong in `server/app/data_pipeline/processed/`.
